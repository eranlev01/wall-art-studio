## Strategic Insights for Our Artist

1. **School projects are underserved in SEO** — both competitors do them but neither has deep content/landing pages optimized for municipal education budgets
2. **Municipality projects have the highest recurring value** — Gani Tikva's 5+ projects from one artist demonstrates the flywheel effect
3. **Restaurant chains are the most common client** — coffee chains and fast food chains are high-frequency buyers
4. **International credibility is a differentiator** — only Itay Nevet shows international work; our artist should highlight any
5. **Live painting is an untapped service page** — both artists do it, neither has a dedicated page
6. **Sports facility murals are growing** — 5 projects between them but no dedicated service page
