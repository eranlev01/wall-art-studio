# Website Content Strategy — Mural & Graffiti Artist

> Based on competitor research, business goals, and Israeli market analysis | June 2026

---

## Strategic Goals

1. **Attract municipalities** — Position as the go-to studio for urban renewal and public space commissions
2. **Attract schools** — Win institutional education budgets through trust signals and safety credentials
3. **Attract large businesses** — Demonstrate ROI and brand transformation capability
4. **Promote large-scale murals** — Showcase scale and technical capability (buildings, exteriors)
5. **Generate WhatsApp inquiries** — All CTAs funnel to WhatsApp with a pre-filled message

---

## Conversion Strategy

### Primary Conversion Path
Wall photo → WhatsApp inquiry → Quote → Project

### Funnel Design
1. **Awareness:** SEO-optimized landing pages + blog content drive organic traffic
2. **Interest:** Portfolio showcases credibility and scope; named clients build trust
3. **Consideration:** Process page reduces friction; FAQ answers objections; pricing guide sets expectations
4. **Intent:** "Get a free consultation" or "Send a photo of your wall" CTA
5. **Action:** WhatsApp deep-link with pre-filled message requesting wall photo

### WhatsApp CTA Formula
The CTA that works best (observed in both competitors):
> "Talk to me on WhatsApp — just send a photo of your wall and we'll start building the concept together."

Pre-fill the WhatsApp message with:
> "Hello, I came to you through the website. I'm interested in a wall mural for [business/school/home]. Here's a photo of the wall: [please attach]."

### SEO Technical Requirements
- Each service/landing page needs 800+ words of unique content
- Meta titles: include primary keyword + location ("ציורי קיר לבית ספר – [Artist Name] | [City]")
- Schema markup: LocalBusiness + CreativeWork types
- Image alt text: all portfolio images tagged with keywords
- Breadcrumb navigation
- XML sitemap
- Internal linking: each page links to 3+ related pages
- Blog articles: minimum 1,200 words, targeting informational intent

### Content Velocity Plan
- Month 1: Core service pages (businesses, schools, municipalities)
- Month 2: City landing pages (Tel Aviv, Jerusalem, Haifa, Ashdod)
- Month 3: Blog articles (10 initial articles)
- Month 4: Residential pages + long-tail content
- Ongoing: 2 blog articles per month

## Municipality-Focused Pitch Strategy

### Page Message Framework
- **Problem:** "Your city has walls that tell the wrong story — blank, grey, inviting vandalism"
- **Solution:** "Professional public art that builds community pride, reduces vandalism, and creates city identity"
- **Proof:** Municipal portfolio — show Gani Tikva model (5+ projects)
- **Process for municipalities:** How to initiate — concept → proposal → budget → execution
- **Budget sources:** Municipal art budget, culture ministry, urban renewal funds
- **Formal process support:** VAT invoice, safety certification, project documentation
- **CTA:** "Schedule a meeting with our team to discuss your city's vision"

---

## School-Focused Pitch Strategy

### Page Message Framework
- **Problem:** "Blank walls in schools communicate nothing to students"
- **Solution:** "Educational murals that express school values, inspire students, and create community pride"
- **Proof:** School portfolio — show named schools, before/after
- **Process for schools:** PTA presentation, principal coordination, holiday timing
- **Budget sources:** PTA funds, municipal education budget, Education Ministry programs
- **Safe materials:** Non-toxic, child-safe paints — certificated
- **CTA:** "Talk to us about your school — free concept proposal available"

---

## Business-Focused Pitch Strategy

### Page Message Framework
- **Problem:** "Your business's walls are a missed opportunity to communicate your brand"
- **Solution:** "A mural that turns your wall into a marketing asset, creates a shareable moment, and makes customers remember you"
- **Proof:** Restaurant chains, retail stores, offices — named clients
- **ROI angle:** "A wall mural works 24/7 without paying rent. Every customer photo shared on social media is free advertising."
- **Fast execution:** Work during off-hours, minimal business disruption
- **CTA:** "Send us a photo of your wall on WhatsApp — we'll create a free mockup"
