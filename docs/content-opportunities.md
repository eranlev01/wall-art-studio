## FAQ Questions (30+)

### General / Process Questions

1. **How does the wall painting process work from start to finish?**
   Topic: Process overview — consultation, sketch, mockup, approval, execution

2. **How long does it take to complete a wall mural?**
   Topic: Timelines — small murals (1-2 days) to large buildings (2-3 weeks)

3. **Do I need to prepare the wall before painting?**
   Topic: Surface preparation, priming, repair requirements

4. **Can I see what the mural will look like before you start painting?**
   Topic: Digital mockup / 3D visualization service

5. **What happens if I want changes during the process?**
   Topic: Revision policy, approval stages, client collaboration

6. **Do you work alone or with a team?**
   Topic: Studio model, collaborative projects, team for large commissions

7. **What areas in Israel do you serve?**
   Topic: Geographic coverage, travel fees, nationwide service

8. **Do you work internationally?**
   Topic: International projects, travel arrangements

9. **How do I start? What do you need from me?**
   Topic: Onboarding — "just send a photo of your wall"

10. **Can you match our brand colors exactly?**
    Topic: Color matching, Pantone/RAL systems, brand guidelines

### Pricing Questions

11. **How much does a wall mural cost?**
    Topic: Pricing factors — size, complexity, surface type, location

12. **Is there a minimum project size or budget?**
    Topic: Minimum order, small murals vs. large commissions

13. **Do you charge by the hour or by the project?**
    Topic: Pricing models, project-based vs. hourly

14. **Are there additional costs I should know about?**
    Topic: Materials, scaffolding, travel, primer, protective coating

15. **Do you offer discounts for non-profits or schools?**
    Topic: NGO pricing, educational institution discounts, municipal rates

16. **Can the mural be paid in installments?**
    Topic: Payment terms, deposit structure, milestone payments

17. **What's included in the price?**
    Topic: What's covered — design, materials, labor, protective finish

### Technical Questions

18. **How long will an outdoor mural last?**
    Topic: Exterior durability, UV-resistant paints, weather resistance, typical lifespan (5-10+ years)

19. **What paint do you use — is it safe for schools and children?**
    Topic: Non-toxic materials, VOC-free paints, safe for educational environments

20. **Can you paint on brick, concrete, metal, or glass?**
    Topic: Surface compatibility, primer requirements, different techniques

21. **Can a mural be removed or painted over later?**
    Topic: Reversibility, overpainting, mural removal

22. **What size can you paint?**
    Topic: Scale capability — from small accent walls to multi-story buildings

23. **Can you work at night or on weekends to avoid disrupting business?**
    Topic: Flexible working hours, minimal business disruption

24. **Do you use spray paint or brushes?**
    Topic: Techniques — spray, brush, airbrush, mixed media

25. **How do I maintain and clean a painted wall?**
    Topic: Aftercare, cleaning instructions, touch-up policy

### Business/Municipality Questions

26. **How do we get the municipality to fund a mural project?**
    Topic: Municipal budget processes, cultural funding sources, how to write a mural proposal

27. **Can you provide a formal quote/invoice for institutional procurement?**
    Topic: Formal bidding, quote documentation, VAT invoice

28. **Do you have liability insurance and safety certification?**
    Topic: Professional credentials, work safety, scaffolding permits

29. **Have you worked with municipalities before? Can you provide references?**
    Topic: Municipal case studies, reference list, portfolio of public works

30. **Can you help design the concept, or do we need to come with an idea?**
    Topic: Full-service vs. execution-only, concept development service

31. **What's the best wall in our space to paint on?**
    Topic: Advisory consultation, wall selection guidance

32. **Can the mural include our school's name or logo?**
    Topic: Text integration, logo painting, institutional branding

33. **Do you create murals for children's rooms?**
    Topic: Residential service, child-safe materials, personalized themes
