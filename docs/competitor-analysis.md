- File attachment on contact form

## Summary Recommendations

Both competitors are strong in portfolio presentation and WhatsApp conversion but **neither has a municipality-dedicated landing page** despite both doing municipal work. This is the single biggest gap to exploit.

Neither has an FAQ page, pricing guide, or case study format. Blog content exists only at Itay Nevet and is basic. There is clear opportunity to create the most comprehensive, authoritative mural artist website in Israel by combining: deep municipality/school/business landing pages, a strong blog, FAQ, pricing guide, video portfolio, and a compelling artist story.
