# Artist Profile

## Experience

15+ years of experience creating graffiti, murals, and large-scale wall art projects across Israel.

The journey started through volunteer community mural projects, local festivals, and collaborations with small businesses. Over the years, the work evolved into professional large-scale projects for businesses, schools, public spaces, festivals, and private clients.

## Mission
